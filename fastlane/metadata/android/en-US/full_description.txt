Neo24 AI is an Android client for the Neo24 AI service.

The app provides access to AI chat and supports text input, speech input, text-to-speech output, camera and image upload, file upload, and user account login.

This F-Droid edition does not include Google Play Billing or native Google Play Services authentication. Authentication is handled through the user's web browser and the Neo24 AI server.

A Neo24 AI account is required for account-based features.

Website: https://neo24.ai
